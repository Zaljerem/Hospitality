1.1.7 - 10/20/2026 - Major update!

- Fixed food-swiping guests failing to approach sleepers, and made them stop if the target wakes before food is taken. **Thanks to mrbeverage!**
- Guests now arrive with 75–100% rest, reducing immediate outdoor sleeping when they cannot afford a bed.
- Improved performance during guest social interactions, shopping, and bed selection.
- Reduced unnecessary work scans when no guests are present.
- Reduced repeated relationship calculations and guest-bed UI work.
- Optimized inventory-capacity checks for Combat Extended compatibility.
- Fixed recruitment-ready notifications, including checks for royal guests.
- Improved handling of temporarily unusable guest beds to help prevent repeated sleep-job failures.
- Fixed Soft Warm Beds compatibility: bedding now survives conversion between normal and guest beds and displays correctly.
- Fixed food-scrounging reservations blocking the food owner's meal.
- Fixed food transfers taking incorrect quantities or merging with nearby ground stacks, and respected guests' carrying limits. **Thanks to mrbeverage for these scrounging fixes!**

1.1.8 - 11/1/2026

- Fixed the default guest area changing from Unrestricted to Home when saving or loading.
- Fixed factions that cannot send peaceful visitors consuming guest scheduling slots and delaying valid visits.
- Fixed Spaceports replacement pickups being requested for guests who had already boarded or left on a recalled shuttle.
- Suppressed guest temperature warnings on space maps, including Odyssey orbital sites with other biomes.
- Fixed Spaceports treating newly recovered guests as incoming shuttle arrivals.
- Improved Spaceports compatibility for guest recruitment and boarding, and arranged separate pickup shuttles for recovered travelers in orbit.
- Orbital guests now retry unavailable Spaceports pickups and receive a replacement if their shuttle is destroyed.
- Fixed orbital guest arrivals during Kessler storms; eligible orbital arrivals still bypass Spaceports' shuttle percentage.
- Recovered travelers now start their own guest visit instead of joining an existing group. 
- Reduced repeated stat calculations while selecting guest beds with no resolved room.
- Improved Ideology guest-bed search performance by reusing facility requirements within each search.
- Reduced vending-machine cache overhead during item spawning and improved cleanup of discarded maps and duplicate registrations.
- Fixed stale relationship icons and tooltips after switching saves, and reduced allocations when refreshing the guest table.
- Fixed guest bed and food thoughts occasionally reusing another pawn's cached result, and improved cache cleanup between games.
